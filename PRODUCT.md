## Platform

web

## Users
- **Recruiters and tech leads** (Brazil and abroad) screening a junior/mid full stack developer. They arrive from LinkedIn, GitHub or a job application, spend 30 to 90 seconds, and decide whether to open the projects or contact him.
- **Freelance clients** checking whether he can deliver an API, integration or web app.

## Product Purpose
Online résumé and portfolio of Eduardo Proença. Success is a visitor opening a live demo or repository and then reaching out (LinkedIn, e-mail, phone).

## Positioning
Full stack developer, **C# / .NET on the backend and TypeScript / React / Next.js on the web**, whose portfolio is a working, connected system rather than tutorials: a .NET API, a Node event-delivery service and a Next.js dashboard that talk to each other in production, each with CI, hundreds of tests and a live demo.

## Operating Context
Remote and async. Time zone UTC−3. Open to full-time remote roles and freelance/contract work.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind CSS 4, deployed on Vercel from `main`.
- Two languages: Portuguese (default) and English, same facts in both, switchable in the page and linkable (`?lang=en`).
- Must stay a fast static page that prints/reads well; content lives in `src/data/resume.ts`.

- No testimonials, client logos, metrics of business impact or certifications exist: never invent them.

## Product Principles
1. Proof over adjectives: every claim links to code or a running demo.
2. Scannable in 30 seconds: role, stack, best project and contact above the fold.
3. Honest seniority: present real experience and dates exactly; never inflate.
4. Bilingual parity: English visitors get the same facts, not a summary.
