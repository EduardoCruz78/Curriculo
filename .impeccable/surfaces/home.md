# Surface: home (résumé, `/` in Portuguese and `/en` in English)

Mode: Persuade. The visitor (recruiter, tech lead, freelance client) decides in 30–90 seconds whether to open a project or get in touch.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a transit network, not a stack of cards: four projects drawn as metro lines whose interchanges are the real integrations between them (the API publishes events to the relay, the relay delivers signed webhooks to the dashboard, the dashboard drives the API). Refuses the dark-navy developer template with a centred name over a grid of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: White signage ground, near-black ink, line colours used at full strength as the only colour: blue (SchedulingApi), green (WebhookRelay), red (SchedulingDashboard), yellow (EduPlatformJS), violet (DesafioBackend). Fixed-width strokes, 45° bends, white station rings with black outlines, round line roundels with a number. One grotesque family (Archivo, expanded widths for station-sign headings), hierarchy by weight and width. No shadows, no gradients, no cards; panels are separated by line strokes and rules.

STORY: Within one screen the visitor reads who he is (Full Stack, C#/.NET + TypeScript), sees that his projects form one working system, and has LinkedIn, e-mail and GitHub within reach. Scrolling, each line becomes a route with its stations (features), its numbers (tests) and two doors: live demo and code. Experience is one more line with dated stations.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with the EP roundel, section links and the PT/EN switch. Left 5/12: name at display size, role line, one sentence of pitch, availability, then the primary action (LinkedIn) and secondary actions (e-mail, GitHub, résumé PDF is out of scope). Right 7/12: the network map, full height of the fold, legend under it. On phones the map comes after the actions and simplifies to the three connected lines.

FORM: Metro / transit network diagram, position 1 of 7 on the grounded list (taken as IMPECCABLE'S PICK; the user delegated the choice, aiming at employability). seed:cf5237e5ec5fc719. Raises kept: one family, hierarchy by weight (from emission-line rail); one line in focus at a time, the rest dim (from the detector display); colour owns whole line regions, never sprinkled (from the single-hue program).
